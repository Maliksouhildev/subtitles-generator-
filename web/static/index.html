Video Editing & Safe-Zone Guide

1

CapCut (Mobile & Desktop)

Add the generated subtitle video as an Overlay above your main video. Select Remove BG > Chroma Key, click the green background with the color picker, and set Intensity to 15-20%.

2

Adobe Premiere Pro

Place the green-screen video on a track above your media. Search for the Ultra Key effect in Effects, apply it to the subtitle clip, and pick the green color using the Eyedropper tool.

3

DaVinci Resolve

In the Edit or Color page, use the 3D Keyer or in Fusion use the Delta Keyer node to key out the green screen background instantly.

4

Black Screen (Screen Blend Mode)

If you select Black Screen, no chroma keying is needed! Simply change the video layer's Blending Mode to Screen in CapCut, Premiere, or Final Cut.

5

TikTok, Reels & Shorts Safe Zones

Always ensure subtitles are positioned at least 260px-320px from the bottom to prevent creator profile handles, follow buttons, captions, and the audio title from obscuring your text.
